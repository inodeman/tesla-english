THE HISTORY OF TESLA | 3D Documentary | 15 minutes

The electric car had already died. More than once. Then a tiny company in California decided people should not have to drive an ugly car to save the planet. They had to want it.

This documentary follows Tesla from the graveyard of early electrics to the founding by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning in 2003, Elon Musk’s arrival, the Roadster, Christmas Eve 2008 — when the company was hours from disappearing — the Model S, the Supercharger network, Model 3 production hell, a stock that became a religion, the Model Y as the world’s best-selling car, the Cybertruck, the Semi, the robotaxi, the Cybercab, and the Roadster Tesla showed on October 15, 2026.

Ten million cars. One brand. And a question that does not fit in a post: did Tesla save the electric car, or did it simply arrive first to a war that had already started?
